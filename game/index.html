OPEN-WORLD CRIME IN LA VEGA

Fresh off the ferry with empty pockets and a fast reputation. Twelve linked jobs across three acts — steal cars, win a street race, run packages, shake the cops, settle the score with the Vultures, pull off the La Vega Score, and finish with the Harbor Heist. A living city, smart cops, and four wild biomes beyond the bay.




Save from the pause menu (P) — progress also auto-saves after each job.

WASD / arrows — move & drive  ·  Shift — run  ·  Space — jump  ·  Q — punch  ·  F — shoot
E — get in / out of a car, talk to La Rosa  ·  P — pause  ·  S — save  ·  M — mute
Tab — weapon wheel (1-5 to pick)  ·  mouse wheel — camera zoom in / out  ·  B — buy (gun shop / crew / business)
On Android: left joystick to move, right buttons to drive & fight.
